# 📊 Walmart Inc. (WMT) Valuation Analysis
**Peer Comparison Project – Self-Directed (2025)**

---

## 🔍 Project Overview
This project analyzes whether Walmart's stock is **overvalued, fairly valued, or undervalued** by comparing its valuation multiples against three key competitors: **Costco (COST), Target (TGT), and Kroger (KR)**.

**Goal:** Apply fundamental valuation techniques to a globally recognized company and deliver a clear, data-backed investment recommendation.


**⚠️ Disclaimer:** I hold a Bachelor's degree in Financial Management and completed this project as part of my professional development. This analysis is for **educational and demonstrative purposes only** and does not constitute financial advice. I am **not a licensed financial advisor**. All data is publicly available and all assumptions are my own.

---

## 📈 Key Findings
| Metric | Walmart (WMT) | Competitor Average | Implication |
|--------|---------------|-------------------|-------------|
| **P/E Ratio** | 39.46x | 33.34x | Walmart trades at an 18% premium |
| **P/B Ratio** | 9.38x | 7.36x | Premium reflects asset efficiency |
| **P/S Ratio** | 1.23x | 0.77x | Market confidence in Walmart's scale |
| **EV/EBITDA** | 19.70x | 16.02x | Strong underlying cash flow quality |

---

## 🎯 Conclusion
**Recommendation: HOLD**
- Walmart is a high-quality business with unmatched scale and stability
- However, it appears **overvalued** relative to peers across all multiples
- A better entry point would be closer to the industry average (P/E ~33x)
